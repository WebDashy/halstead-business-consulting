Services / Business Strategy Consulting

A plan shaped around where your business actually is

A strategy deck that doesn't account for your real constraints — cash, headcount, market position — is a document nobody implements. We start with where you are, not where a template assumes you should be.

Book a Consultation

What's included

  • A working session on your business's actual constraints and goals before any strategy is drafted
  • Market and competitive analysis grounded in your real customer base, not generic industry benchmarks
  • A phased strategy roadmap with concrete milestones, not a five-year vision statement
  • Financial modeling to pressure-test the plan against your actual cash position
  • Implementation support through the first 90 days, not a handoff at the final presentation
  • A single advisor accountable for the strategy from kickoff to results review

Typical timeline

A standard strategy engagement typically runs 6–10 weeks from kickoff to final roadmap, with implementation support extending as needed. We'll give you an exact schedule based on your business's complexity.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with businesses of any size?

We focus on companies with 10–250 employees — established enough to have real constraints, small enough that one advisor can actually understand the whole business.

What happens after the strategy is delivered?

We stay engaged through initial implementation and offer ongoing advisory retainers for businesses that want continued support.

Can you work alongside our existing leadership team?

Yes — most engagements are designed to work with your team, not around them. We're brought in to add capacity and outside perspective, not replace decisions that are yours to make.
