Frequently asked questions

Do we need an in-house finance team already?

No — many of our clients don't have a dedicated finance hire yet. We build the model to be maintained by whoever handles your books, even if that's an outsourced bookkeeper.

Can you work with our existing accounting software?

Yes — we build models that pull from QuickBooks, Xero, and most standard accounting platforms.

Is this useful if we're not raising outside capital?

Yes — a rolling forecast is just as valuable for a self-funded business managing its own cash runway as it is for one preparing a board deck.
