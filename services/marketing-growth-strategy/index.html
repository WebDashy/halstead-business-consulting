What's included

  • Customer research grounded in interviews with your actual buyers, not assumed personas
  • Positioning and messaging that differentiates against your real competitive set
  • Channel strategy prioritized by where your buyers actually spend attention, not every channel at once
  • A measurement framework so you know what's working within weeks, not a year
  • Content and campaign planning aligned with your sales cycle length

Typical timeline

A standard marketing strategy engagement typically runs 5–8 weeks from research to final channel plan. We can also structure ongoing advisory support through initial campaign execution.

Frequently asked questions

Do you execute the marketing, or just the strategy?

We specialize in strategy and can recommend execution partners, or work alongside your existing marketing team or agency to implement the plan.

How many customer interviews are typically involved?

Usually 8–15 interviews, enough to identify real patterns without dragging the research phase out for months.

Can this work for a B2B business, not just consumer brands?

Yes — most of our marketing strategy work is actually B2B, where the buyer research and channel prioritization matter even more given longer sales cycles.
