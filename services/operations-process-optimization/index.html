Services / Operations & Process Optimization

Workflows redesigned around how your team actually works

An org chart rewrite doesn't fix a broken handoff between two departments. We map the actual workflow first, then fix what's actually slowing it down.

Book a Consultation

What's included

  • Process mapping sessions with the people who actually do the work, not just department heads
  • Identification of the specific handoffs and bottlenecks causing delays or rework
  • Redesigned workflows with clear ownership at each step
  • Tooling recommendations where the right software would remove manual busywork — never a mandate to buy anything specific
  • A phased rollout plan so changes don't disrupt operations mid-implementation
  • Follow-up review 60–90 days after rollout to confirm the changes stuck

Typical timeline

A standard process optimization engagement typically runs 6–8 weeks from initial mapping to rollout plan, with a follow-up review 60–90 days after implementation.

Frequently asked questions

Will this involve layoffs or restructuring?

Not by default — most process work is about removing friction and duplicated effort, not headcount reduction. We'll be direct with you if a specific finding does point toward a staffing change.

How do you handle employee pushback during process mapping?

We frame every session around understanding the work, not auditing performance — this consistently gets more honest input than a top-down review would.

Can you focus on just one department instead of the whole company?

Yes — many engagements start with a single problem area like fulfillment or customer onboarding rather than a company-wide overhaul.
